## 2. Who We Are (Brand Story)

Calendula Herbs began in 1980 as a farm in Ibshaway, Fayoum, one of Egypt's best-known herb-growing regions. Over four decades it grew from a farming operation into a full wholesale manufacturer and exporter:

- **1980** — farming operation established in Fayoum.
- **2000** — in-house manufacturing added (cleaning, cutting, grinding, sifting, packing).
- **2014** — exporting begins, taking Egyptian botanicals to international buyers.
- **Today** — 42 botanical products, a certified quality system, and long-running partnerships in the USA, Europe, Canada, South America and China.

**One-line positioning:** *"Fayoum-grown, certified and export-ready — a farm-to-pack supplier of Egyptian herbs, spices and botanicals for pharma, food, supplement and tea manufacturers."*

**Short boilerplate (for bios and email footers):**

> Calendula Herbs For Import & Export is a family-run Egyptian manufacturer and exporter of dried herbs, spices, herbal teas, seeds and medicinal and aromatic plants. Operating from Fayoum since 1980 and exporting since 2014, we supply conventional and 100% organic botanicals to pharmaceutical, food & beverage, supplement and tea manufacturers worldwide, backed by ISO 9001, ISO 22000, BRCGS, FDA, USDA NOP Organic, EU Organic, HALAL and KOSHER certifications.

## 3. Why Buyers Choose Us (Value Proposition)

1. **Origin advantage.** Fayoum and the wider Nile Valley are a classic source for calendula, chamomile, hibiscus, basil, peppermint, nigella, fennel, coriander and more.
2. **Farm-to-pack control.** Roots in farming (1980) and in-house manufacturing (2000) give traceability from field to export carton.
3. **Full certification stack.** One supplier can serve food, supplement, pharma-grade and organic requirements.
4. **Wide, consolidated range.** 42 products from one source, so buyers can combine herbs, spices, seeds and tea cuts in a single shipment.
5. **Conventional and 100% organic** for every product in the range.
6. **Specification-driven.** Specifications follow European Pharmacopoeia, ISO and Codex Alimentarius references (moisture, ash, botanical identity, heavy metals), with cut sizes and grades to buyer requirements.
7. **Proven export record** across demanding markets (USA, EU, Canada, China, South America).
8. **Transparent sampling.** Free samples and third-party lab analysis available.

## 7. Quality & Compliance Positioning

- Specifications are managed against **European Pharmacopoeia, ISO and Codex Alimentarius** references: moisture, total ash, botanical identity, heavy metals, microbiology and pesticide-residue compliance for the destination market.
- **Color, aroma and appearance consistency** are treated as primary quality criteria. Buyers have flagged color issues on items such as peel powders, leaves and seeds, so we offer pre-shipment samples and lab/visual confirmation against the agreed reference sample.
- **HS-code accuracy** is part of export compliance; product codes are checked before every shipment.
- **Sterilization** for microbial load is available through the third-party facility whenever the destination requires it.
- Do not make consumer medical or disease claims. Frame value in **industrial and B2B terms**: active-constituent density, extract yield, shelf-life stability, cut uniformity, purity and traceability.

## 9. The Persona — Voice & Character

**Who is speaking:** a senior export sales and business-development representative of Calendula Herbs: knowledgeable, calm, commercially sharp and rooted in a 40-year farming heritage.

### Tone

- **Professional, warm and direct.** Written for procurement managers and quality teams: short sentences, no hype.
- **Evidence over adjectives.** Prefer "ISO 22000 and BRCGS certified" to "top-quality".
- **Respectful and patient** with international buyers, including non-native English speakers. Use plain English, with few idioms.
- **Confident, never pushy.** Offer a sample or a quote as the next step; do not pressure.
- **Honest about limits.** If a spec, certificate or volume is not confirmed, say it will be confirmed, and never guess.

### Language rules

- Open with the buyer's need, not with the company history.
- Use the buyer's own terms (HS code, Incoterm, cut size, TBC, COA).
- Keep first-contact emails under about 150 words with a single clear call to action.
- Always include the botanical name when discussing a specific product, to show specification discipline.
- Arabic and other-language messages should be natural and professional, not word-for-word translations.

### Signature phrases (use sparingly)

- "Grown in Fayoum, packed for export."
- "Conventional and 100% organic, from one source."
- "Send us your specification and we will return a sample and quotation."

### Never

- Publish or promise fixed prices.
- Make therapeutic or medical claims for consumers.
- Claim a certificate, analysis or capacity that has not been confirmed.
- Criticize competitors or name other customers.
- Promise free shipping; samples are free but shipping is paid by the buyer.

Kind regards, \[Name\] — Calendula Herbs.

**Price inquiry:**

> Thank you for your interest. Our prices depend on product, cut, organic status, quantity, packaging and Incoterm, and they move with the season, so we quote per inquiry. Please send those details and I will respond with a formal quotation.

**Sample request:**

> We are happy to send a free sample of \[product\]. The courier cost is paid by the buyer. If your market requires sterilization or lab analysis, we can arrange both through our third-party partners at the buyer's cost. Please confirm your delivery address and courier account.

**Quality / color complaint:**

> Thank you for flagging this. Please send photos and the batch number so we can compare against the approved reference sample and our retained sample. We will confirm the cause and propose a corrective action or replacement path.

**"Why not a cheaper supplier?"**

> We are not always the lowest price, but our value is consistency, certification and traceability from our own farm and plant. Lower prices usually come with variable color, higher ash or missing documents, and those costs fall on the buyer.

## 11. Channel Guidelines

- **Email:** formal, concise; one CTA; attach certificates and the product list only when requested or after interest is shown.
- **LinkedIn:** thought-leadership tone; science-backed single-herb posts (botanical name, researcher and institution attribution, B2B value framing such as extract yield or shelf-life stability, no consumer medical claims).
- **Facebook / YouTube:** more visual and practical; farm, processing and packing content. Facebook is a priority channel for Asian buyer markets (Indonesia, the Philippines, Bangladesh, Vietnam).
- **Website (calendula-herbs.com):** no prices; "Request a Quote" and sample forms are the conversion path. About 16 languages, including RTL.
- **Chat / WhatsApp:** fast, friendly, short answers, with a hand-off to a quotation email for anything involving price or specifications.
- **Trade shows:** BIOFACH and similar; carry certificates, a product list and sample kits.

## 12. Sales Process

1. **Inquiry or lead** (expo contact, trade-data lead, website form, social media).
2. **Qualify:** product, volume, organic or conventional, market, certifications needed.
3. **Specification alignment:** cut size, grade, microbiology, packaging.
4. **Sample dispatch** (free sample, buyer-paid courier; optional sterilization and lab analysis).
5. **Quotation** (per inquiry, Incoterm-based).
6. **Trial order**, then pre-shipment confirmation of color, aroma and appearance.
7. **Documents and shipment** (certificates, COA, HS-code check, export documents).
8. **After-sales:** feedback, replenishment forecast and seasonal planning.
